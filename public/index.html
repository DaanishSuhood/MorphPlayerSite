Plays almost anything. On every screen you own.

MorphPlayer is a media player built on LibVLC, the engine behind VLC. MKV, MP4, AVI, WebM, FLAC, MP3, Opus, HLS and RTSP streams: it plays them on Windows, on Android TV and on Android phones, with the same subtitle matching, resume and themes on each.

The same player everywhere

The three apps share their rules, so a film behaves the same on the laptop, the TV and the phone.

Plays almost anything

LibVLC does the decoding, so there are no codec packs to install. Hardware decoding handles 4K and HEVC smoothly on TV boxes and phones.

Finds your subtitles

Not just Movie.en.srt: subtitles from another release of the same film, 1x02 for S01E02, or a Subs folder. All found, named by language, and the best one turned on.

Carries on where you left off

Files longer than 3 minutes start where you stopped, with Start over on offer. Positions near either end aren't kept, as in VLC.

Folders become playlists

Open a file and the rest of its folder plays after it, in the order you'd expect: Episode 2 before Episode 10. Shuffle plays everything once before repeating.

Tracks, speed and sound

Audio and subtitle tracks by language, 0.25× to 4× with the pitch kept, and stereo modes including a headphone mix of 5.1 and 7.1 sound.

Windows

MorphPlayer for Windows

A minimal window with an auto-hiding control bar, a playlist panel and a right-click menu for everything else. Drop files or folders on it to play them.

  • Website links: paste a YouTube link (or any site yt-dlp supports) and it streams, at the quality you choose, from 360p to 4K. MorphPlayer fetches yt-dlp and keeps it up to date.
  • Playlist thumbnails from posters beside your files, covers inside them, or a frame from the video.
  • Seek bar previews: hover over the bar to see the frame there.
  • Audio devices: switch between speakers, headphones and Bluetooth without stopping.
  • One window: files opened from Explorer join the window that's already open.

Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit. Nothing else to install: no .NET, no VLC. The installer or the portable zip, as you prefer.

Android TV

MorphPlayerTV

For Android TV and Google TV boxes, such as the Xiaomi Mi Box. Plug in a USB drive, pick a film, and its folder becomes the playlist.

  • Made for the remote: OK pauses, left and right seek (further the longer you hold), Menu opens the options.
  • Send a link from your phone: the home screen shows an address and a QR code; open it on your phone, paste a stream link, and it plays on the TV.
  • Continue watching on the home screen, with how far you got.
  • Audio passthrough sends Dolby and DTS untouched to a soundbar or receiver.
  • Films keep their shape: black bars stay where they belong, even on boxes whose decoders report the wrong size.

Android TV 7.0 or later, 32-bit and 64-bit ARM. Install it with the free Downloader app; see below.

Android phones and tablets

MorphPlayerMobile

The same player with a touch interface. Your videos and music are found wherever they are, in Camera, Download, WhatsApp or a memory card, and listed by folder.

  • Gestures: double-tap the sides to skip 10 seconds, swipe for brightness, volume and seeking, pinch to fill the screen, and a lock for when it's in a pocket.
  • Music in the background, with lock-screen and headset controls and a mini player.
  • Picture-in-picture: leave the app and the video shrinks into a small window.
  • Turns to suit the video: films sideways, upright videos upright.
  • Open with and Share from your file manager, or a message with a stream link in it.

Android 8.0 or later, 32-bit and 64-bit ARM.

Installing

The apps aren't in the Microsoft Store or Google Play; they come straight from their GitHub releases. Each one then offers its own updates, checked against published SHA-256 checksums.

Windows

  1. Download the installer above and run it.
  2. MorphPlayer isn't code-signed, so SmartScreen may warn you: choose More info → Run anyway.
  3. To open videos by double-clicking, pick MorphPlayer in Settings → Apps → Default apps.

Android TV

  1. On the box, install Downloader (by AFTVnews) from the Play Store.
  2. In Downloader, enter the APK's address from the TV card above, and allow it to install apps when asked.
  3. Open MorphPlayerTV from the apps row.

Phone or tablet

  1. Open this page on the phone and tap Download APK.
  2. Open the download, and allow your browser to install apps when Android asks.
  3. Open MorphPlayer from your apps.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. All three apps are open source under the MIT licence, with no ads, accounts, tracking or paid features. On their own, they only ask GitHub once a day whether a newer release is out (and the Windows app updates yt-dlp), which you can turn off.

Why isn't it in the Play Store?

It's a personal project, installed from its releases. Google Play also restricts two things the apps rely on: All files access, which is how subtitle files beside your videos are found, and updating themselves.

Why can't the Android apps play YouTube links?

The libraries that read YouTube on Android are under the GPL licence, which would rule out the apps' MIT licence. The Windows app plays them through yt-dlp, a separate program it downloads for you.

Why doesn't Android show my subtitle files to the app?

With only the media permission, Android shows an app videos and music and hides every other file. Give the app All files access when it asks (on many TV boxes this needs one adb command, shown in the app), or add a subtitle file by hand from the subtitles menu on the phone.

Is it VLC?

It uses LibVLC, VLC's playback engine, so it plays what VLC plays. Everything around the engine is MorphPlayer's own. MorphPlayer isn't made or endorsed by VideoLAN.

Where do I report a problem?

Open an issue on the app's GitHub page: Windows, Android TV or phone.
